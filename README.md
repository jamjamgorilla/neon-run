# Neon Run

An endless rooftop runner through a rainy neon city. Jump, double jump and slide. It keeps getting faster.

**Play it:** https://jamjamgorilla.github.io/neon-run/

## Controls
- Jump: Space, W, Up arrow or tap (again in the air to double jump, hold for higher)
- Slide: S, Down arrow or swipe down
- P: pause, M: sound on or off

One HTML file: drawing, sounds and saving are all in `index.html`. Your best score is saved in your browser.
